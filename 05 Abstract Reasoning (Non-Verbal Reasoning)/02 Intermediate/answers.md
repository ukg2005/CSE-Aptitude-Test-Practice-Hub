## Number Sequence Questions (1–20)

1. **Solution**:
   Differences: 3 - 1 = 2, 6 - 3 = 3, 10 - 6 = 4, 15 - 10 = 5. Differences increase by 1.  
   Next difference = 6. Next: 15 + 6 = 21.  
   **Answer**: 21

   **Answer**: 95
