## Data Sufficiency

71. **Solution**:
    (1) \( x + 2 = 7 \), so \( x = 5 \). Not sufficient (x = 5, not > 5).  
    (2) \( x - 3 = 2 \), so \( x = 5 \). Not sufficient.  
    Both give \( x = 5 \). Not sufficient.  
    **Answer**: Not sufficient


72. **Solution**:
    (1) \( y + 4 = 10 \), so \( y = 6 \). Sufficient.  
    (2) \( 2y = 12 \), so \( y = 6 \). Sufficient.  
    **Answer**: Each alone is sufficient
